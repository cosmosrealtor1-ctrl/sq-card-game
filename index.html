How to play

  1. 4 players, 13 cards each. Arrange them yourself into 4 sets of 3 cards; 1 card is left over (Extra). Drag a card onto another to swap (or tap two cards). You have 60 seconds – when time runs out your sets are locked in as arranged.
  2. Set ranking, strongest first:
    1. Trail – three of a kind (AAA best, then KKK …)
    2. Pure Sequence (same suit) – A‑K‑J › 2‑3‑5 › A‑K‑Q › A‑2‑3 › K‑Q‑J › Q‑J‑10 › J‑10‑9 › 10‑9‑8 › 9‑8‑7 › 8‑7‑6 › 7‑6‑5 › 6‑5‑4 › 5‑4‑3 › 4‑3‑2
    3. Sequence (mixed suits) – same order: A‑K‑J › 2‑3‑5 › A‑K‑Q › A‑2‑3 › K‑Q‑J › … › 4‑3‑2
       Every Pure Sequence beats every mixed Sequence.
    4. Color – 3 cards of the same suit (e.g. A‑9‑5 ♥)
    5. Pair – two the same
    6. High Card – highest single card
  3. Picture chart: open ranking chart (if the image file is in the same folder).
  4. Your 4 boxes are Slot 1–4. By default they auto-order strongest → weakest when you press Ready. Untick Auto-order to set your own order with ◀ ▶ or by dragging a box's ⠿ handle.
  5. Edit Sets: any time during the round (no time limit) you can rearrange the cards in your unthrown sets and the Extra card. Thrown sets are locked, and the drop rule still compares against what you already threw. Press Done Editing before you throw.
  6. Throw timer: when it is your turn to throw you have 30 s (Easy) / 25 s (Medium) / 20 s (Hard). If time runs out, your next set in slot order is thrown automatically (even if that makes you drop). The timer keeps running while you use Edit Sets.
  7. 4 turns per round. Everyone throws one set per turn. Biggest set wins the turn = 1 point, and the winner throws last next turn (the player after the winner leads).
  8. If sets are equal, the later thrower wins.
  9. You must throw your sets biggest first, then 2nd, 3rd, 4th. If you throw a set bigger than one you already threw, you DROP: out of the round, and you lose that round's points.
  10. Win all 4 turns in a round = 6 points (4 + 2 bonus).
  11. Four of a kind: the game does not tell you. Press 🀄 Four of a Kind before your first throw: 6 points, the round is not played. If two players announce four of a kind, the higher four wins the 6 points (A highest) and the other gets nothing; if only one announces, that one wins. Not pressed = chance lost.
  12. 6 pairs (6 different pairs, e.g. 7‑7, 9‑9, J‑J …): press 👯 Six Pairs before your first throw: 6 points, the round is not played. Not pressed = chance lost.
  13. 3 trails (three different three-of-a-kinds in your 13 cards, e.g. 8‑8‑8, 7‑7‑7, 6‑6‑6): press 🔱 Three Trails before your first throw: 6 points, the round is not played. Not pressed = chance lost.
  14. Wrong Four of a Kind, Six Pairs or Three Trails claim: you lose 3 points, every other player gets +1, and play continues.
  15. 5 pairs – Call Draw: the game does not tell you. If you have 5 pairs you may press 🎴 Call Draw any time before your first throw of the round: the round is cancelled, no points, new deal. If you call Draw without 5 pairs: you lose 3 points and every other player gets +1, and the round is cancelled. If you don't call, the round just continues. Computer players call Draw only when they really have 5 pairs and a weak hand (no Sequence or Trail).
  16. Priority: four of a kind first, then 6 pairs, then 3 trails. If two players have 6 pairs (or two have 3 trails), the one who throws first in that round wins. Three of a kind counts as one pair.
  17. Whenever someone gets these 6 points, a screen shows who it was, what they had and their cards – also when those points end the match.
  18. DROP penalty: a player who drops is out of the round, scores nothing for it, loses 3 points, and every other player gets +1. Scores can go below zero.
  19. First to 13 points wins the match.

The game

  • You play against 3 computer players.
  • You get 13 cards. Arrange them into 4 sets of 3 cards, plus 1 extra card.
  • To move cards: drag a card onto another card, or tap two cards to swap them.
  • Finish before the timer runs out. Each throw also has a timer.
  • Throw your strongest set first, then the weaker ones.
  • The biggest set on the table wins the turn and gets a point.
  • The first player to reach 13 points wins.

Your name

Write your name (up to 12 letters). Empty = You.

Level

Easy / Medium / Hard: choose how strong the computer players are.
